What the numbers show

Maharashtra's economy grew 7.9% in real terms in 2025-26, after 7.3% in 2024-25. At current prices its GSDP reached ₹51.01 lakh crore.

Since 2011-12, the economy has grown 4.0 times at current prices and 2.25 times after adjusting for prices, an average real growth of 6.0% a year. The only year of real contraction was 2020-21 (−9.5%).

In 2024-25, the latest year with figures for all 30 reporting states and UTs, Maharashtra ranked 1st by GSDP, 1.48 times the size of second-placed Tamil Nadu (₹31.19 lakh crore).

Income per person tells a different story. Maharashtra's per-capita NSDP was ₹3,47,903 in 2025-26, and in 2024-25 it ranked 9th of 30.

7 · Source & method

Source and method

Dataset
National Accounts Statistics: State GSDP, NSDP and per-capita NSDP (MoSPI, eSankhyiki)
Base year
2011-12. MoSPI has moved national GDP to a 2022-23 base; state series on the new base are not yet published. Figures here are not comparable with the new national series.
Prices
“Current prices” are in each year's rupees. “2011-12 prices” remove inflation and are used for real growth.
Coverage
30 states and UTs report. Ranks use 2024-25, the latest year with all of them; 2025-26 has 15 so far.
Revisions
Recent years are often revised in later releases. We keep every release and log changes on the corrections page.
Next update
When MoSPI publishes revised state estimates.
